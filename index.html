About Me

I am a Geospatial Data Scientist and Full stack developer with 17+ years of experience developing Earth-observation-driven decision-support systems for environmental and climate risk monitoring applications. I specialize in converting complex environmental and climate datasets into standardized and API-enabled data infrastructures using Django, Flask, FAST API and Nodejs. I have experience in designing geospatial ETL pipelines and satellite time-series analytics using Google Earth Engine, Python, and R; spatial databases ( Postgres/PostGIS & mongoDb) and web-based geospatial liraries (Leaflet, and GeoServer). I have experience and with international organizations such as the World Bank, UNICEF, UN-WFP,RCMRD and SADC.

Services

What Can I Deliver?

I design and deploy Earth Observation-driven geospatial systems, analytics pipelines, and decision-support platforms for climate, environmental and disaster risk management.

EO Analytics & Climate Risk Modeling

Development of satellite-based drought, flood, crop and climate risk analytics using Google Earth Engine, Python and R. Design of automated workflows generating NDVI, SPI, rainfall anomalies and impact indicators for operational monitoring.

Geospatial Data Engineering & APIs

Design of geospatial ETL pipelines transforming raw satellite and climate datasets into analysis-ready infrastructures. Development of REST APIs (Django, Flask) and spatial databases (PostGIS) powering scalable geospatial platforms.

Web-based Decision Support Systems

Development of interactive web-based geospatial dashboards using Leaflet, GeoServer and modern frontend frameworks. Supporting governments and international organizations with operational early warning and risk monitoring systems.
