About Me

I am a Geospatial Data Scientist and Full stack developer with 17+ years of experience developing Earth observation driven decision support systems for humanitarian, environmental and climate risk monitoring applications. I specialize in converting complex environmental and climate datasets into standardized and API-enabled data infrastructures using Django, Flask, FAST API and Nodejs. I have experience in designing geospatial ETL pipelines and satellite time-series analytics using Google Earth Engine, Python, and R; spatial databases ( Postgres/PostGIS & mongoDb) and web-based geospatial liraries (Leaflet, and GeoServer). I have experience and with international organizations such as the World Bank, UNICEF, UN-WFP,RCMRD and SADC.

AgroClimate Intelligence Dashboard

A production-ready geospatial decision-support platform that transforms Earth Observation data into actionable insights for drought monitoring, agricultural risk analysis, and climate early warning in Zimbabwe. Designed to bridge the gap between remote sensing analytics and operational humanitarian decision-making.

Key Features
Multi-Source Earth Observation Integration
  • Integrates CHIRPS rainfall, MODIS NDVI, ERA5 temperature
  • Time-enabled raster visualization with dynamic layer controls
Near Real-Time Geospatial Visualization
  • Interactive Leaflet maps with choropleth and raster layers
  • Province-level aggregation and ranking
Anomaly Detection & Early Warning System
  • Computes deviations from long-term baselines:
    • rainfall_monthly_baseline
    • ndvi_monthly_baseline
    • ndvi_anomaly
    • Vegetation Condition Index
  • Identifies climate stress signals and emerging drought conditions
  • Converts satellite data into early warning indicators
User-Driven Spatial Analytics (AOI-Based Analysis)
  • Interactive polygon drawing tools for custom Area of Interest (AOI)
  • On-demand computation of:
    • Mean rainfall and NDVI
    • Area-based statistics
  • Outputs:
    • Time-series charts (trend analysis)
    • Downloadable CSV datasets
Reporting & Decision Support
  • Automated generation of analytical outputs and visual reports
  • Designed for integration into early warning systems
  • Supports humanitarian reporting workflows
Technical Architecture
  • Backend: Django, Python REST APIs, Google Earth Engine (GEE)
  • Database: PostgreSQL with PostGIS
  • Frontend: Leaflet, JavaScript, HTML/CSS
  • Data Pipelines: Automated ingestion and processing of satellite time-series data
