About Me

Geospatial Data Scientist and Earth Observation Systems Architect with 17+ years of experience designing and operationalizing satellite-driven decision-support systems for climate risk, disaster risk reduction, and humanitarian intelligence. Complemented by 4+ years of full-stack geospatial engineering experience, I specialize in translating complex Earth observation and environmental datasets into scalable, API-driven geospatial infrastructures and operational intelligence platforms. I have led the design of national and regional early warning systems for droughts, floods, and food insecurity across Africa, working with organizations including UNICEF, WFP, World Bank, FAO, African Union Commission, EU, and SADC. My expertise spans end-to-end geospatial system development—from satellite data processing and geospatial ETL pipelines to cloud-based analytics platforms, REST APIs, and interactive decision-support dashboards. Technically, I combine Google Earth Engine, Python, R, and machine learning with modern geospatial engineering stacks (PostGIS, Django/Flask/FastAPI, GeoServer, Leaflet, and Docker) to deliver scalable, production-ready Earth observation systems. My work focuses on transforming geospatial intelligence into actionable insights for policy, humanitarian response, and climate adaptation. Selected for the FAO Consultancy Roster (2025–2028) as a Remote Sensing & GIS Specialist, I continue to support international programs focused on climate resilience, anticipatory action, and geospatial digital transformation.

🌍 Global Resilience Hub – Integrated Geospatial Monitoring Platform

A full‑stack Django platform powered by Google Earth Engine (GEE) for global‑scale computing and per‑country geospatial analysis. The system processes and visualises climate, environmental, food security, and disaster risk indicators across any country or region.

Key Features
  • GEE‑driven indicators: rainfall (CHIRPS/GSMaP), NDVI (MODIS/Sentinel), soil moisture (ESA CCI), land surface temperature (MODIS)
  • Global computing & per‑country analysis: run zonal statistics, generate tile layers, and extract summaries for any ADM0/ADM1/ADM2
  • Interactive Leaflet maps with dynamic GEE‑generated raster tiles
  • Country filtering and custom date range (start/end) for temporal analysis
  • Toggleable vector overlays: boundaries, rivers, roads, health facilities, schools, markets, livelihood zones, IPC phases
  • REST API endpoints serving GeoJSON and GEE tile URLs
Technical Architecture
  • Computing Engine: Google Earth Engine (Python API) – all raster analysis, reduction, tile generation
  • Backend API: Django, Django REST Framework (orchestrates GEE calls, caches results, serves GeoJSON)
  • Frontend: Leaflet.js, vanilla JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
  • Tiling / Raster: Dynamic tile endpoints (e.g., rainfall, NDVI)
Analytical Capabilities
  • Multi‑layer situational awareness (climate + food security + risk)
  • Anomaly detection via indicator time‑series
  • Regional aggregation (ADM1, ADM2 pop‑ups)
  • Tile endpoints that return GEE visualisations as ready‑to‑use map layers
  • Export‑ready GeoJSON overlays for offline analysis

Consulting Services

Geospatial Intelligence & Earth Observation Solutions
